# Test Case TC-07: Out-of-Order Answers

## Objective
Verify that the agent remembers answers provided before their corresponding questions.

## Simulated Conversation

Agent: Could you tell me what type of property you own?

Customer: It's a residential house. I am the sole owner, and I have the original documents. I need ₹25 lakh. I'm salaried and receive my income through my bank account. The property is worth around ₹60 lakh, and I prefer a 10-year tenure.

## Expected Result

The agent identifies and records all seven data points:
1. Property type: Residential.
2. Ownership: Sole.
3. Original documents: Available.
4. Loan amount: ₹25,00,000.
5. Occupation/income mode: Salaried, bank income.
6. Market value: ₹60,00,000.
7. Tenure: 10 years.

The agent must not repeat questions that have already been answered clearly.

If all answers are valid, the agent may proceed to the eligibility evaluation stage. It must not ask redundant questions or qualify the customer before evaluating every condition.
