# Test Case TC-06: Busy Customer

## Objective
Verify that the agent offers a callback instead of continuing qualification.

## Simulated Conversation

Agent: Good morning! My name is Kumar, and I'm calling from Home Credit. May I speak with the intended customer?

Customer: Yes, that's me, but I'm driving right now. Can you call tomorrow afternoon?

Agent: Of course. What time tomorrow afternoon would be convenient for you?

Customer: Around 3 PM.

Agent: Thank you. I've recorded your preferred callback time. Have a good day.

## Expected Result

The agent acknowledges the customer's availability, captures the requested callback time if supported, and ends the current call without continuing eligibility questions.

The agent must not claim a callback has been scheduled unless the platform or connected scheduling system confirms it. Otherwise, record the requested callback time for follow-up.
